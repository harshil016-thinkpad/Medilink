Understanding Malaria: Prevention, Symptoms, and Treatment

Malaria remains a critical global health challenge, caused by Plasmodium parasites and transmitted through infected female Anopheles mosquitoes. Understanding its symptoms, prevention strategies, and treatment options is vital for saving lives and protecting communities. Read More

Understanding Tuberculosis: Transmission, Symptoms, and Care

Tuberculosis remains one of the world's leading infectious public health threats. Caused by bacteria that primarily target the lungs, TB spreads through the air when an infected individual coughs or sneezes. Recognizing early symptoms like prolonged coughing and night sweats is essential for timely, effective treatment Read More

Understanding Ebola: Transmission, Symptoms, and Response

Ebola virus disease is a severe, often fatal illness transmitted to humans from wild animals and spread through direct contact with infected bodily fluids. FAQs

What services are provided at this health facility?

→ We offer outpatient consultation, emergency care, maternal and child health services, laboratory diagnostics, immunization, routine health screenings, and pharmacy services.

What should I bring when visiting or admitting a patient?

→ Please bring a valid photo ID, your health insurance card or patient ID card (if registered), previous medical records or referral forms, and a list of current medications you or the patient are taking.

What are the operating hours for outpatient and emergency services?

→ Outpatient clinics run from Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Our emergency department and inpatient services operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

How can I schedule or reschedule an appointment?

→ You can schedule or reschedule appointments by calling our main line, visiting our reception desk in person, or using the online appointment booking portal on our website.

What infection prevention and safety measures are enforced on-site?

→ To protect patients and staff, all visitors must sanitize their hands upon entry, wear face masks in designated clinical areas, and adhere to posted physical distancing and hand hygiene guidelines.

How are medical emergencies handled if I arrive after clinic hours?

→ Our Emergency Unit is fully staffed around the clock. Proceed directly to the emergency entrance, where triage staff will assess and prioritize care based on urgency.
